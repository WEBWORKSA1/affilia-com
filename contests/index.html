What to submit

A case study about promoting any affiliate program, published on your own site, channel or newsletter between August 1, 2026 and January 31, 2027. Articles, videos and long social threads are all welcome. It should cover:

  • Which program and why you chose it
  • What you published and where traffic came from
  • Real numbers: clicks, conversions, earnings or whatever you can share
  • What worked, what didn’t, and what you’d change

How entries are judged

CriterionWeight
Usefulness to other publishers40%
Transparency of numbers and method30%
Clarity20%
Originality10%

Big numbers don’t win by themselves. A clear account of a modest result that teaches something beats a vague boast.
