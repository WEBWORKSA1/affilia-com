About Affilia

Affilia exists because affiliate terms are hard to compare and easy to misread. We put the numbers side by side, build tools that do the arithmetic, and explain the mechanics plainly, for the publishers who promote and the brands who pay.

What we publish

  • A program directory with published commission terms, payout models, cookie windows and tracking platforms, checked against primary sources.
  • A network and software comparison using vendors’ published pricing.
  • Free tools: calculators, a link builder, a disclosure generator, a program matchmaker and a commission planner for brands.
  • The Academy: practical guides on how programs pay, how attribution works, disclosure rules, content, SEO, and running a program.
  • Videos and industry headlines, curated and refreshed automatically.

The name

“Affilia” is a short form of affiliation: the relationship between a brand and the partners who recommend it. Affilia.com is an independent publication and is not connected with any organization that uses a similar name. See our trademark and copyright notice.

How we are funded

Affilia is free to use. We earn money from display advertising served by Google AdSense, from clearly labeled sponsorships and featured listings, from introductions we make for brands that ask for them, and from reader support. None of these change the terms we publish or the conclusions we reach. Our editorial policy and affiliate disclosure explain the safeguards.
