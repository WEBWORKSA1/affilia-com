Semrush affiliate program

Semrush is an SEO and online-visibility suite used by marketers and agencies. Its affiliate program runs on Impact and pays fixed amounts for new subscriptions and free trials, with last-click attribution and a 120-day cookie.

Commission
$200 per new subscription plus $10 per trial (published range $100 to $450 by toolkit)
Cookie window
120 days
Payout model
One-time
Runs on
Impact

Who it suits

SEO bloggers, YouTube tutorial channels and agency audiences who already do keyword research and site audits. Trial bounties pay even when a reader doesn’t subscribe, which smooths income from tutorial content.

Content that tends to convert

  • Keyword-research tutorials that use the free trial
  • Site-audit case studies run on a real website
  • Tool comparisons for in-house teams and agencies

Watch-outs

Payouts are one-time, so a customer who stays five years still earns you a single bounty. Last-click attribution means a later click on someone else’s link takes the sale.
