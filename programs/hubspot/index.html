HubSpot affiliate program

HubSpot sells a CRM platform with marketing, sales, service and content tools, from a free tier up to enterprise plans. Its program pays a share of every referred subscription for the first year, with a six-month cookie that suits long B2B buying cycles.

Who it suits

Publishers whose readers run or market a business: B2B blogs, agency owners, marketing educators and SaaS reviewers. The free CRM gives readers a low-risk first step, and paid upgrades are where commission builds.

Content that tends to convert

  • Migration guides for teams outgrowing spreadsheets or a basic CRM
  • Walkthroughs of free HubSpot tools that lead naturally to paid hubs
  • Honest comparisons with Pipedrive, Zoho and other CRMs

Watch-outs

Commission stops after 12 months, and readers who stay on the free tier pay nothing. HubSpot changes its pricing pages often, so link to them rather than quoting prices.
