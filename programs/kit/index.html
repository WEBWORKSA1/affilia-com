Kit affiliate program

Kit, formerly ConvertKit, is email marketing software for creators and newsletter businesses. Its program pays a tiered recurring commission that keeps paying after the first year.

Who it suits

Creators, bloggers, podcasters and course sellers whose audiences are building email lists. Newsletter-growth content is its natural home.

Content that tends to convert

  • How to start a newsletter, from first subscriber to first sponsor
  • Migration guides from other email tools
  • Creator income breakdowns that show the tool at work

Watch-outs

Kit has changed its tiers and rates in recent years, so read the current terms before quoting a number. The calculator on this page uses a conservative 20% rate.

Before you apply
