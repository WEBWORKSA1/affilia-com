Shopify affiliate program

Shopify is an e-commerce platform for starting and running online stores. Its program runs on Impact and pays a fixed bounty for each referral who buys a full-price plan, with a lower bounty in some regions.

Commission
$150 per full-price plan referral ($25 bounty in some regions)
Cookie window
Set by the program
Payout model
One-time

Impact

Who it suits

E-commerce educators, dropshipping and side-business channels, and agencies that build stores for clients.

Content that tends to convert

  • How to start an online store, from product idea to first sale
  • Niche store case studies with real numbers
  • App and theme recommendations for specific store types

Watch-outs

Bounties depend on the region and the plan bought. Trial users who never upgrade to a paid plan earn nothing.
