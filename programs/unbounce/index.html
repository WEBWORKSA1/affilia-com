Unbounce affiliate program

Unbounce builds landing pages and conversion tools for marketers who pay for traffic. Its program runs on PartnerStack and pays 25% to 35% recurring for up to 12 months, with a 90-day cookie that renews on repeat clicks.

Who it suits

PPC managers, conversion-rate specialists and agencies that build campaign pages. Readers who already spend on ads understand what a better landing page is worth.

Content that tends to convert

  • Landing-page teardowns with before-and-after numbers
  • Templates for specific campaigns such as webinars or lead magnets
  • A/B testing tutorials for small budgets

Watch-outs

Recurring commission ends after a year. Higher-priced plans carry most of the value, so aim at teams rather than hobbyists.
