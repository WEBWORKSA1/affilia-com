Amazon Associates

Amazon Associates pays a percentage of qualifying purchases, with the rate set by product category. The standard window is 24 hours after a click, extended to 90 days for items added to the cart within that day.

Commission
0% to 20% of qualifying purchases, by category
Cookie window
24 hours (90 days for carted items)
Payout model
One-time
Runs on
Amazon Associates

Who it suits

Product reviewers, gift-guide publishers and hobby sites whose readers are ready to buy. Amazon’s checkout converts well, and you earn on everything in the basket, not only the item you linked.

Content that tends to convert

  • Best-product round-ups based on real testing
  • Seasonal gift guides
  • Kit lists for a hobby, craft or profession

Watch-outs

Low category rates and a short window mean you need volume. New accounts must make three qualifying sales within 180 days to stay active, and the operating agreement has strict rules on how links and prices are shown. Amazon also requires the statement “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases” on your site.
