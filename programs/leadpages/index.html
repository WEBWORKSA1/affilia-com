Who it suits

Coaches, consultants and small-business marketers who need simple pages without a developer. It fits audiences that choose tools on ease of use rather than feature lists.

Content that tends to convert

  • Lead-magnet funnels for coaches and consultants
  • Simple pages for local service businesses
  • Comparisons with all-in-one funnel builders

Watch-outs

Small businesses cancel faster than larger teams, which shortens recurring income. Feature comparisons date quickly; review them twice a year.
