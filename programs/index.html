How to read this directory

Commission is what the program says it pays, in its own terms. A percentage applies to the customer’s payment; a dollar figure is a fixed bounty per qualifying sale or signup.

Model tells you when money arrives. Recurring programs pay on renewals, either for a set number of months or for the life of the account. One-time programs pay once per customer. Hybrid programs combine a bounty with a smaller recurring share, or pay one rate on the first sale and another on renewals.

Cookie is the attribution window: how long after a click a purchase still counts as yours. Long windows matter most for products people research for weeks, such as business software and hosting.

Runs on names the platform that tracks clicks and pays you. Programs on Impact or PartnerStack need an account on that platform; official programs usually run on the brand’s own software.
