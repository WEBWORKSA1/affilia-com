Free tools for affiliate partnerships

Six tools that do the arithmetic and the paperwork. They run entirely in your browser: no account, and nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere unless you choose to email yourself a result.

For publishers and creators

  • Commission calculatorMonth-by-month earnings for percentage, bounty and hybrid programs, with recurring terms, cancellations and refunds.
  • EPC and RPM calculatorTurn earnings, clicks and pageviews into earnings per click and per thousand views, then compare with your AdSense RPM.
  • Affiliate link builderAdd an affiliate ID and UTM tags to any URL and get HTML with the rel attributes search engines expect.
  • Disclosure generatorShort disclosures for posts, videos, email and social, plus a full disclosure page you can publish.
  • Program matchmakerAnswer four questions about your audience and get six programs ranked by fit.

For brands

  • Brand commission plannerFind the commission rate your margin and acquisition target can afford, and what the program will cost each month.

Why the numbers matter more than the rate

A 50% commission on a $10 product pays less than 20% on a $99 subscription that renews for a year. Headline rates are marketing; the money depends on order value, how long payments recur, how many customers cancel and how many clicks convert. Each tool here makes one of those variables visible so you can compare programs on what they would actually pay you.

Every result is an estimate built on the inputs you give it. Use your own analytics where you have them, and treat any projection as a planning aid rather than a promise.
