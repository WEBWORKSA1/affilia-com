The networks that aggregate brands and affiliates. Top 5 drive over 70% of industry revenue, but no single player exceeds 10% share. Consolidation is accelerating.

Impact

"Built for big retail. Heavy onboarding. Worth it at scale."

Global network now incorporating ShareASale (merged 2025). Strong for retail, fashion, travel. ~$19B GMV / $1.4B publisher revenue annually.

PRICING $1+ depositBEST FOR Retail / E-commerce

"Shed its budget reputation post-ShareASale merger. Strong global reach."

B2B SaaS partnership platform. Single-login marketplace, centralized payouts, server-to-server tracking. Used by 116k+ active partners.

PRICING $500+/moBEST FOR B2B SaaS

"The SaaS-native winner. Marketplace access alone justifies the price for growth-stage."

"Niche champion. If your brand fits, there's no better network."
