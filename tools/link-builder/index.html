Why the rel attribute matters

Google asks site owners to mark paid and affiliate links with rel="sponsored", and also accepts rel="nofollow". Marking links this way tells search engines not to treat them as editorial endorsements, which protects your site from link-scheme penalties. The snippet includes both, plus noopener for safe new-tab links.

UTM tags and affiliate IDs do different jobs

Your affiliate ID tells the program who to pay. UTM tags tell analytics tools where a visitor came from. Some programs strip unknown parameters or forbid UTM tags on their links; if your clicks stop tracking, remove the UTM fields and test again.

Keep links honest

Cloaking a link so readers can’t see where it goes is allowed by many programs, but it never replaces disclosure. Place a short disclosure before the first affiliate link on the page. The disclosure generator writes one for you.
