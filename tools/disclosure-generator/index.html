Where to put it

  • Web pages: before the first affiliate link, near the top. A footer link alone is not enough.
  • Video: say it out loud near the start, and repeat it in the description above the “more” cutoff.
  • Email: at the top of any issue that contains affiliate links.
  • Social: at the start of the caption, not buried in hashtags.
  • Podcasts: read it before the offer and again when you give the link.
