Cookie policy

Effective November 6, 2026.

Cookies are small files that websites store in your browser. This page lists what Affilia.com and the services it uses store, and how you can control it.

What we store on your device

NamePurposeKept for
affilia-themeRemembers light or dark themeUntil you clear it
affilia-cookiesRemembers that you dismissed the cookie noticeUntil you clear it
affilia-nudgeLimits the brand growth-plan prompt to once every 14 daysUntil you clear it
affilia-landing, affilia-referrer, affilia-utmRecords how you arrived, attached to forms you submitYour browser session

These are stored with your browser’s local and session storage, are necessary for the features above, and are not used for advertising.

Advertising cookies from Google

Google AdSense and its partners use cookies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance and, where permitted, personalize them based on your visits to this and other sites. Google publishes details in How Google uses cookies in advertising.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalized ads are shown only after you consent through a Google-certified consent message.

Embedded videos and fonts

YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play; YouTube may then set its own cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google and do not set cookies.

How to control cookies

  • Change ad personalization in Google’s Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of some vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works, though you will see the cookie notice again and ads may be less relevant.
