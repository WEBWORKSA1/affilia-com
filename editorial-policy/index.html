Editorial policy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

What we promise readers

We publish what programs actually offer, explain it plainly, and tell you when money is involved. Accuracy and independence come before speed, traffic or revenue.

How programs are chosen

A program is listed when it publishes terms we can verify, sells something a reader could reasonably want, and has a track record of paying partners according to independent affiliate communities. We exclude programs for products that are illegal or deceptive, or that Google’s publisher policies prohibit. Listing is free; payment never buys a listing that fails these criteria.

How terms are verified

We check commission, payout model, cookie window and tracking platform against the program’s own pages first, then network listings and independent directories. Each program page states when its terms were last checked. Where sources disagree or a program doesn’t publish a figure, we say so rather than guess. We review the directory on a monthly cycle and whenever readers or programs report a change.

Sponsored content and paid placement

Featured listings, sponsorships and sponsored guides are labeled where they appear. Sponsors never see or approve our verdicts in advance, and payment never changes the terms or data we display. Display ads are served by Google AdSense, selected by Google rather than by us, and kept visually separate from editorial content.

Use of software and AI tools

We use software, including AI tools, to help research, draft, check calculations and maintain the site. Every page is reviewed by an editor who checks facts against sources before publication, and editors are responsible for everything we publish.

Earnings examples

Calculators and worked examples illustrate how commission terms translate into money. They are not predictions or promises. We avoid income claims, and we don’t publish testimonials we can’t verify.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and update the date on the page. Material corrections are noted on the page itself. Report an error through the contact page; we act on credible reports within five business days.

Independence

No advertiser, sponsor, supporter or listed program has a say in what we write. If a conflict of interest arises, we disclose it on the affected page.
