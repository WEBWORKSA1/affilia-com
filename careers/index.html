How we work

  • Evidence over opinion. Every claim we publish has a source or a test behind it.
  • Clear briefs, fast feedback. You’ll know what good looks like before you start.
  • Paid on time. Invoices are paid promptly through common international methods.
  • Credit. Writers and creators are credited on their work.

Hiring process

  1. Send the form with a work sample.
  2. Short paid trial project for shortlisted applicants.
  3. Ongoing work as projects open.

We consider every applicant on the quality of their work, regardless of background, location, age, gender, disability, religion or any other personal characteristic.
