The Thesis

Affiliate is moving from links and SEO to citations and conversations. Most networks aren't built for it.

The 25-year-old affiliate playbook — write product reviews, rank on Google, place tracked links — is breaking in real time. The brands and partners that thrive in 2026 are operating on completely different rails.

01 / The decay

Google AI Overviews now intercept 1 in 5 commercial queries.

Independent affiliate sites have lost 40–70% of clicks since AI Overviews launched. Reddit, Forbes, and three media holding companies own most of what's left of the SERP. The old model is structurally broken.

93% zero-click rate · −47% YoY traffic to mid-market affiliate content
02 / The opportunity

AI search converts 5× better than Google. The audience is already there.

800M weekly active ChatGPT users. Perplexity referrals convert at 11× organic search rates. The buyers haven't disappeared — they've moved to interfaces where most affiliate programs have zero presence.

14.2% AI conversion · 2.8% Google · The gap is the opportunity
03 / The wedge

Citation engineering is a different game than SEO. Networks haven't pivoted.

LLMs cite Wikipedia, Reddit, G2, YouTube, Substack. None of those are SEO plays. None of the major affiliate networks have rebuilt their publisher base around them. We have. That's the wedge.

7.8% Wikipedia · 1.8% Reddit · 1.1% G2 — citation share by source
