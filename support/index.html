Keep Affilia independent

Affilia is free to read and free to use. Ads cover part of the cost. Reader support pays for the rest, and it keeps our program data honest because no single sponsor pays the bills.

What supporters get

  • $5 and up: our thanks, and the knowledge that the tools stay free.
  • $25 and up: your name on the supporters list, if you want it there.
  • $100 and up: early access to new tools and a say in what we build next.
  • $500 and up: a founding-supporter credit and a direct line to the editorial team.

The fine print

Affilia.com is an independent publication, not a charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible. Support doesn’t buy editorial influence: supporters, sponsors and advertisers are all held to the same editorial policy.

Brands that want visibility in return should look at sponsorships or sponsoring a contest prize.
