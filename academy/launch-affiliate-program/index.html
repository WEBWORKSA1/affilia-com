Launch an affiliate program in 90 days

By the Affilia editorial team. Updated November 6, 2026. An 8-minute read.

Most affiliate programs fail quietly. They launch, sign up a few hundred partners who never send a sale, and get written off as “not our channel.” The ones that work treat the first 90 days as a recruiting and activation project, not a software installation.

Days 1 to 30: economics, terms and plumbing

Set the economics first

Decide the most you will pay to acquire a customer, then work back to a commission. The commission planner does this in a minute, and the commission rates guide explains the reasoning. Leave headroom for bonuses; you will need them.

Write terms partners can trust

  • Cookie window that matches your buying cycle.
  • Validation period and what counts as a reversal.
  • Payout threshold, schedule and methods.
  • Prohibited methods: bidding on your brand name, unauthorized coupon codes, incentivized clicks, spam.
  • Disclosure requirements, so partners comply with FTC rules.

Choose the platform

Use a network if you need to find partners; use software if you already know who they are. The networks versus in-house guide has the break-even math. Then test tracking end to end: click, buy, refund, and confirm each shows up correctly.

Build the partner kit

Product facts, approved claims, logos and screenshots, a few ready-made comparison tables, and landing pages that convert. Partners promote what is easy to promote.

Days 31 to 60: recruit the right twenty

Two hundred inactive partners are worth less than twenty active ones. Recruit in this order:

  1. Customers and fans who already recommend you. They convert best and need the least persuasion.
  2. Complementary products whose users need yours. Integration partners often refer steadily.
  3. Content publishers already ranking for “best” and “alternatives” searches in your category.
  4. Newsletters and YouTube creators whose audience matches your buyer.

Write to each one personally. Say why their audience fits, what you will pay, and what you will give them to make promotion easy.

Days 61 to 90: activate and learn

  • First-sale bonus. A small extra payment for a partner’s first sale breaks the inertia that kills most programs.
  • Monthly partner update. New features, best-converting pages, upcoming promotions.
  • Tiers. Higher rates for partners who pass a monthly threshold reward the ones who matter.
  • Fraud checks. Review new partners’ traffic and conversion patterns before payouts. See the fraud guide.
